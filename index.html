Your expertise, our shared mission

Our mission is to promote Chinese culinary culture worldwide—and that mission comes to life through the people who work here. Whether you join brand and sales, R&D, digital technology, supply chain, manufacturing or a corporate function, you will see how your role connects to real consumer and customer needs, collaborate across markets, and contribute to outcomes that reach communities around the world. This is the employee proposition at Lee Kum Kee: a clear line from what we stand for to what you can build in your career.

03 How We Work
CMS · Benefit card grid · 4 items

Support for every stage

People do their best work when they have the support to grow and thrive. Depending on role and location, Lee Kum Kee offers competitive reward, health and wellbeing support, learning and development, and policies that help employees navigate important stages of life.

ICON

Competitive reward

Salary and incentive structures aligned to your role, performance and local market—plus recognition programmes that celebrate contribution and achievement.

ICON

Health & wellbeing

Medical coverage, regular health check-ups, and employee assistance programmes to support your physical, mental and emotional wellbeing.

ICON

Learning & development

Structured training, mentoring and on-the-job experiences that help you build capability, explore new functions and progress in your career.

ICON

Family & life support

From parental leave to family-friendly policies, plus additional resources to support your family needs at important stages of life.

Benefits, eligibility and policy details vary by role and location.
